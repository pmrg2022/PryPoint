What is PryPoint?

PryPoint is a VR game made by me (Pmrg2022/PryPoint), it uses BIMOS as a starting point of the game, but has been changed a lot by now. PryPoint will be free until version 1.000, currently it is at version 0.112. When it becomes paid it will be $0.99.

PryPoint is a work in progress, and will be in development for a while.

Development

PryPoint is an independently developed project.

PryPoint has been in development for about 3 months now.

Platform

PryPoint is currently developed and tested for Meta Quest 3. Other devices may be supported, such as Pico and other Quest headsets.
