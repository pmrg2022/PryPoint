Installation

1

Download PryPoint from one of the listings above.

2

Go to files, and hold trigger on the file until the menu pops up.

3

Click "open with", then open with package installer.

4

Click install, wait, continue with the install menu.

5

When done, either click "done", or "open".

6

If you want to open after exiting, open the menu, go to the side, click the menu, then go to "Unknown sources".

7

Then click PryPoint, this will open the game.
